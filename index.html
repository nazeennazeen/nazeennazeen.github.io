About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Lebanese French University with a passion for creating innovative digital solutions that combine technology, creativity, and design. As the top-ranked student in the Faculty of Engineering and Computer Science, I enjoy transforming ideas into impactful projects through software development, UI design, digital marketing, and visual storytelling.

Beyond academics, I have led media teams, managed social media platforms, and created engaging digital content for organizations and communities. My technical background includes web development with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and C++, along with experience in MATLAB and Microsoft Office. I also specialize in graphic design using Adobe Photoshop, where I create branding materials, promotional content, and creative visual designs.

I believe the best technology is built by combining technical expertise with creativity, effective communication, and problem-solving. I'm always eager to learn, collaborate, and contribute to projects that make a meaningful impact.
